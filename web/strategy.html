Vorax Helper · Strategy guide

The playbook

Built from tens of thousands of simulated runs where the bot only ever sees what you would see: your tanks and the cards on offer. Numbers are for the realistic game (random tanks, random offers, Shelley level 6).

Instruments: the part that matters most

Instruments are the biggest lever, and they are chosen at the 8,000 and 28,000 Ultimate Activity thresholds, so the pick can't be redone.

  1. First instrument: take Incubation Sac. Claws is an acceptable substitute. If neither is offered, refresh (you have two refreshes).
  2. Second instrument: refresh until Lady of the Plague Quarter or Beak shows up.
  3. Third instrument: Sac + Madonna wants Beak; Sac + Beak wants Lady of the Plague Quarter; Claws + Madonna wants Canine Teeth File or Pig Iron Bone Saw.
Avoid Aberrant Stinger, Nettle Toggle and Two-Inch Skull Nail. They consistently lower the chance of a big finish.

Potions

Potion choices are rarely close, so the one-round-ahead answer in the analyzer is usually right. When two options are within a few points, run the deep check: it plays each one to the end of the run and reports the chance of reaching your target, with a confidence range.

Two findings worth knowing:

  • Build tables are a ceiling, not a plan. Pairs ranked with both instruments equipped from round 1 overrate Claws. Always validate in the dealt (realistic) mode, which is what this guide does.
  • Tanks matter less than you'd think for a single pick, but they decide how much a given potion is worth. That's why the analyzer asks for your exact board.

Unproven: a two-round lookahead bot looked about 5 points better at 600k, within noise on 300 games. It needs around 1,500 games to confirm.
